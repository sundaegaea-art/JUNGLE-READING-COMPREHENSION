How to Play

  1. 1 Read the story carefully on the passage screen. Take your time!
  2. 2 Answer five questions about the story using multiple choice options.
  3. 3 Earn stars and collect rewards for every correct answer you submit.
  4. 4 Complete all five jungle reading challenges to become the Ultimate Jungle Reading Champion!

About the Game

Jungle Readers: Reading Adventure! is specifically crafted for Grade 3 elementary pupils (ages 8-9). It combines engaging storytelling with comprehension challenges to make reading fun, interactive, and rewarding!
